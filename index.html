About Ashford & Briggs

We believe that exceptional staffing is both an art and a science.

At Ashford & Briggs, we've developed proprietary AI-powered technology that transforms how organizations connect with talent. Our cultural assessment platform goes beyond resumes and interviews to understand the intricate dynamics that make teams successful.

Using advanced AI-driven phone screening, we prequalify candidates efficiently and accurately, evaluating fit before they ever meet your team. Our 20-point career personality assessment evaluates candidates across dimensions that traditional hiring overlooks—from collaboration styles and communication preferences to values alignment and growth trajectories.

We're building partnerships with forward-thinking companies who understand that their people are their greatest asset, and who refuse to settle for anything less than exceptional fit.
