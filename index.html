Your AI Advantage on Every Call

Paladin Veritai gives you the intelligence edge — before, during, and after every recruiting conversation

Pre-Call Skills Gap

Enter the candidate's LinkedIn URL, resume, and job description. Paladin produces a skills gap analysis before you ever pick up the phone — so you know exactly where to probe.

Live Prompts

As the conversation unfolds, Paladin generates targeted questions to surface truth, probe inconsistencies, and dig deeper — delivered to your screen in real time. No note-taking needed.

Real-Time Context

Technical terms, industry jargon, and complex topics are defined on your screen as they come up in conversation — so you're never caught off guard.

Post-Call Summary

When the call ends, Paladin analyzes the full conversation and produces a candidate summary with confidence scores for skills match and culture fit. Candidates for the same role are automatically ranked — from top choice to not recommended. Client-ready and shareable before the first real interview.

How It Works

Four steps from your dashboard to a client-ready deliverable.

01

Prepare

Enter the candidate's LinkedIn URL, resume, and job description. Paladin produces a skills gap analysis on your dashboard before the call begins.

02

Connect

Click to initiate the call. Paladin rings your phone first — when you pick up, it dials the candidate and connects you both.

03

Intelligence

Paladin listens to both sides and delivers real-time prompts, insights, and context to the Intelligence Pane on your dashboard. To the candidate, the call is completely transparent — just a normal phone call.

04

Deliver

After the call, Paladin produces a client-ready summary with skills gap analysis and a confidence score to share before the first real interview.
