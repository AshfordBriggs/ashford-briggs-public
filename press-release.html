Ashford & Briggs Launches Paladin: Revolutionary AI Counter-Intelligence Platform for Recruiting Calls

First-of-its-kind telephone-based AI system delivers real-time candidate intelligence without changing recruiter workflows

Jacksonville, FL — March 26, 2026

Ashford & Briggs today announced the pre-launch of Paladin, the recruiting industry's first AI-powered intelligence platform designed exclusively for telephone-based candidate interactions. As 74% of resumes now contain AI-generated content and 65% of hiring managers report encountering deceptive AI use during interviews, Paladin Veritai provides recruiters with the technological advantage they need to combat candidate deception while strengthening authentic human connections.

Addressing the Industry's Growing Deception Crisis

The recruiting landscape has fundamentally shifted. Recent studies reveal that 34% of recruiter time is now spent filtering unqualified or deceptive candidates, while the average cost of a failed hire within 90 days reaches $17,000. By 2028, experts predict 25% of candidate interactions will involve AI-generated or AI-assisted deception.

"In a world racing to replace human interaction with AI, we're strengthening the human relationships that recruiting was built on. Paladin doesn't change how recruiters work — it makes them exponentially more effective at what they already do best."

— Matthew Barker, Co-Founder, Ashford & Briggs

Revolutionary Telephone-Based AI Intelligence

Unlike traditional video conferencing solutions, Paladin operates exclusively through standard telephone systems, making it the only platform of its kind to provide AI-powered recruiting intelligence via regular phone calls. The system requires no app downloads or workflow changes, seamlessly integrating into existing recruiter practices.

Key features include:

  • Pre-Call Skills Gap Analysis: Enter a candidate's LinkedIn URL, resume, and job description to receive comprehensive skills gap analysis before the call begins
  • Live Prompts: Real-time generation of targeted questions to probe inconsistencies and surface truth during conversations
  • Real-Time Context: Instant definitions of technical terms and industry jargon as they arise in conversation
  • Post-Call Summary: Client-ready candidate summaries with confidence scores for skills match and culture fit

Seamless Four-Step Process

Paladin's intuitive workflow maintains recruiter autonomy while delivering unprecedented intelligence:

  1. Prepare: Upload candidate information for pre-call analysis
  2. Connect: System initiates call by ringing recruiter first, then connecting to candidate
  3. Intelligence: AI listens to both sides, delivering real-time insights and prompts
  4. Deliver: Automated post-call summary ready for client presentation

Creative Innovation Meets Practical Application

The platform represents a creative approach to a universal challenge — providing recruiters with AI-powered counter-intelligence without disrupting the personal touch that defines successful recruiting relationships. By focusing exclusively on telephone interactions, Paladin ensures accessibility across all recruitment scenarios while maintaining the natural flow of human conversation.

Market Validation and Future Impact

With recruiting professionals increasingly challenged by sophisticated candidate deception tools, Paladin arrives as a purpose-built solution for the recruiter side of the equation. The platform's telephone-first approach addresses the reality that many critical recruiting conversations still happen via traditional phone calls, particularly in executive search and specialized recruitment scenarios.
